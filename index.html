Welcome to Wow Brows.

brows, lashes & smooth skin

On Mile End Road in Bethnal Green, Wow Brows by Nagina is a beauty parlour known for expert threading, waxing and brow and lash treatments. From a quick eyebrow shape to a full set of lashes, every visit is friendly and precise.

Clients say it best: great service, a lovely experience, and threading and waxing they trust enough to recommend to friends and family. Men’s treatments and manicures are available too.
